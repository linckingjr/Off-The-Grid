Booking Policy

This Booking Policy is a working draft and is not yet a final binding agreement. It is intended for internal review and approval before OTG accepts payments, issues confirmations or publishes final terms.

1. Enquiry-first process

Every booking begins as an enquiry. OTG may request operational details, preferred dates, group size and participant information before confirming suitability and availability.

2. Confirmation

Any booking is only confirmed after OTG sends a written confirmation and the approved payment or deposit process is completed, if applicable. Until then, the proposal remains provisional and subject to change.

3. Deposits and payment

Any deposit or payment conditions must be approved in advance. This website does not currently include a live payment flow and should not be described as offering final online bookings until the full system has been correctly tested.

4. Accurate information

Guests must provide accurate details including group size, contact information, accessibility needs and any special requirements that may affect the experience.

5. Amendments

OTG may adjust dates, locations or itinerary items when necessary for safety, logistics or operational reasons. Final amendment terms must be approved before they are used in a production environment.

6. No live booking claims

At this stage, any booking language on the website must be treated as inquiry-only unless a complete end-to-end booking system has been tested and approved.

Final booking terms, payment conditions and confirmation wording require OTG approval before the site can claim a completed online booking workflow.
