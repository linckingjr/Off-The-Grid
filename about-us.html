About OTG

We design escapes that pull people out of routine and back into the moment.

Off The Grid Events creates experiences shaped around nature, connection and space to breathe. We are not here to overload the schedule or chase noise. We curate trips, gatherings and escapes that feel considered, warm and genuinely restorative.

Every experience is designed to help people feel more present. Whether it is a weekend reset, a couples retreat, a team departure or a custom stay, the goal is the same: help people connect with each other and with the landscape around them.

The OTG approach

Thoughtful, grounded and invitation-led.

  • We build experiences around pacing, atmosphere and intentional downtime rather than rushed itineraries.
  • We focus on group fit, comfort and clarity so guests feel looked after before they even arrive.
  • We keep the process personal and inquiry-led, because the right experience changes depending on the people involved.
