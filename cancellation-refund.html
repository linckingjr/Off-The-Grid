Cancellation and Refund Policy

This Cancellation and Refund Policy is a draft framework intended for approval before publication. It does not reflect a final legal commitment and is not a substitute for a reviewed commercial policy.

1. Cancellation by guest

Guests may request a cancellation or amendment to an enquiry or booking request. Final cancellation windows, refund percentages and administrative fees must be approved before they are openly communicated.

2. Cancellation by OTG

OTG may cancel an experience if operational, safety, venue, weather or logistical conditions make it impractical to proceed. In such cases, the final refund or reschedule policy must be clearly approved and explained.

3. Payment and deposits

Any refund of deposits or payments is subject to the approved payment process, local banking arrangements and the final booking terms adopted by OTG. The current website does not yet operate a live payment or booking system.

4. Force majeure

OTG may be unable to proceed in cases outside its reasonable control, such as severe weather, travel disruption, venue issues or public safety concerns. Any force majeure wording requires final legal review before public use.

5. Final approval required

Final cancellation and refund conditions should be confirmed by OTG before they are published. This page remains draft content until approved.

This cancellation and refund draft should be reviewed by OTG and legal/commercial approvers before being treated as official policy.
