Privacy Policy

Off The Grid Events is preparing this Privacy Policy as a draft document for business review and final approval before publication. This version is not yet a final legal commitment and may require revision based on legal, operational and data-handling requirements.

1. Information we may collect

We may collect personal information provided through enquiries, email correspondence, booking requests, phone conversations, or website forms, including your name, contact details, event interests and any information you choose to share in your inquiry.

2. How we use information

We may use your information to respond to enquiries, confirm suitability for experiences, assess tentative availability, communicate details about an event or trip, and manage general customer support.

3. Security and retention

We will aim to store personal information securely and only for as long as is necessary to manage our enquiries and business operations. Any customer information or payment data must not be committed to public repositories or storage systems that are not approved for production use.

4. Third parties

We may share information with trusted suppliers, service providers or legal advisors where required to provide an experience or manage a booking. Any partner or payment provider must be approved before implementation and before collecting or processing customer data in a live environment.

5. Cookies and analytics

Our website may use basic analytics or third-party scripts to understand use of the site and support improvement of the customer experience. Any analytics or tracking service must be documented, approved and configured to respect privacy requirements.

6. Your rights

Where applicable, you may request to review, update or remove personal information processed in connection with an enquiry or booking. Please note that legal, operational or audit requirements may require us to retain some records.

7. Contact

Official contact details for OTG must be confirmed and approved before publication. Until then, public contact channels should remain empty or managed through the site configuration file rather than being added as live business details.

This document is a working draft and requires OTG owner approval before being treated as a final policy or legal document.
