Who can join

Individuals, Couples and Groups of Friends are all welcome to join OTG.

Our main clients are individuals, couples, solo travellers and groups of friends, private groups (family gatherings & school reunions), and corporate, public and private companies. The aim is to make travelling easy, affordable and accessible to everyone, with thoughtful planning and a strong focus on nature, connection and comfort.

Our guides and bush chefs are highly trained personnel who love what they do, placing customer safety and security first.
