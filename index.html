Who can join

Individuals, Couples and Groups of Friends are all welcome to join OTG.

Our main clients are individuals, couples, solo travellers and groups of friends, private groups (family gatherings & school reunions), and corporate, public and private companies. The aim is to make travelling easy, affordable and accessible to everyone, with thoughtful planning and a strong focus on nature, connection and comfort.

Our guides and bush chefs are highly trained personnel who love what they do, placing customer safety and security first.

About OTG

We create outdoor experiences that help people step away, slow down and feel present.

Off The Grid Events curates immersive adventures shaped around nature, connection and intentional downtime. Our focus is simple: create a sense of calm, energy and belonging without overcomplicating the experience.

Every enquiry is reviewed individually so the trip, stay or activity matches the group, the goal and the pace you actually want. This is an inquiry-first experience: we confirm availability and the right fit before any booking is finalised.

How enquiries and bookings work

  • Send your request through the form below or contact us once final launch details are approved.
  • We check availability, suitability and practical details before confirming a reservation.
  • No payment or booking is finalised on this website until a verified backend and approved policy is in place.
